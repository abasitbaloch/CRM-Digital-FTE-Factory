# TechCorp SaaS - Brand Voice Guidelines

## Core Brand Voice Principles

TechCorp SaaS communicates with **clarity, warmth, and professionalism**. We're helpful experts who respect our customers' time and intelligence.

### Voice Attributes
- **Helpful, not robotic:** We're here to solve problems, not just follow scripts
- **Professional, not stuffy:** We're experts, but we don't use jargon unnecessarily
- **Warm, not overly casual:** Friendly and approachable, but not unprofessional
- **Concise, not curt:** We respect customers' time but remain personable
- **Confident, not arrogant:** We know our product, but we listen and adapt

## Channel-Specific Guidelines

### Gmail (Email)
**Tone:** Formal, professional, detailed

**Structure:**
1. Greeting with customer name
2. Acknowledge their question/issue
3. Provide detailed answer with step-by-step instructions if needed
4. Offer additional help
5. Professional sign-off

**Example:**
```
Hi Sarah,

Thanks for reaching out! I'd be happy to help you add team members to your project.

Here's how to do it:

1. Open your project in TaskFlow Pro
2. Click the "Team" icon in the top right corner
3. Click "Add Members"
4. Enter email addresses or select from your workspace members
5. Choose their permission level (Viewer, Editor, or Admin)
6. Click "Send Invitations"

Your team members will receive an email invitation and can access the project immediately after accepting.

Is there anything else I can help you with?

Best regards,
TechCorp Support Team
```

**Do:**
- Use proper grammar and punctuation
- Include detailed explanations
- Provide links to relevant documentation
- Use bullet points or numbered lists for clarity
- Sign off professionally

**Don't:**
- Use emojis or excessive exclamation points
- Be overly casual ("Hey!" "Awesome!" "Cool!")
- Use abbreviations (write "you" not "u")
- Leave out context or assume knowledge

### WhatsApp
**Tone:** Concise, friendly, conversational

**Structure:**
1. Brief greeting (optional - can skip if customer is clearly in a hurry)
2. Direct answer to question
3. Quick follow-up if needed

**Example:**
```
Hi Mike! Sorry to hear about the app crashes.

Try these steps:
1. Update to latest version (App Store)
2. Restart your iPhone
3. Clear app cache (Settings > Apps > TaskFlow)

Still crashing? Let me know and I'll escalate to our tech team.
```

**Do:**
- Keep messages short (2-4 sentences ideal)
- Get straight to the point
- Use simple language
- Break complex answers into multiple messages if needed
- Match customer's energy level (if they're brief, be brief)

**Don't:**
- Send long paragraphs
- Use formal business language
- Include lengthy explanations unless asked
- Send multiple messages in rapid succession (combine related info)
- Use emojis excessively (1-2 max, and only if appropriate)

**WhatsApp-Specific Tips:**
- Respond quickly (customers expect faster replies on messaging apps)
- Use line breaks for readability
- If answer is complex, offer to send email with full details
- Acknowledge urgency if customer indicates it

---

### Web Form
**Tone:** Semi-formal, balanced, helpful

**Structure:**
1. Greeting with customer name
2. Direct answer to question
3. Additional context if helpful
4. Clear next steps or call-to-action

**Example:**
```
Hi Emma,

Thanks for reaching out about your billing question.

I can see the confusion. You're currently on the Professional plan ($24/user/month), not the Starter plan. The $288 charge is for 12 users at the Professional rate.

If you'd like to downgrade to the Starter plan ($12/user/month for up to 10 users), I can help with that. Just note that you'll lose access to some Professional features like advanced automation and priority support.

Would you like me to process a downgrade, or would you prefer to keep the Professional plan?

Let me know how you'd like to proceed.

Best,
TechCorp Support
```

**Do:**
- Balance professionalism with approachability
- Provide clear explanations
- Offer options when relevant
- Include next steps
- Use a conversational but polished tone

**Don't:**
- Be as formal as email (no need for "Dear" or "Sincerely")
- Be as casual as WhatsApp
- Assume customer context (they may have submitted form days ago)
- Leave questions unanswered

## Handling Different Situations

### Positive Feedback
**All Channels:**
- Express genuine appreciation
- Keep it brief
- Personalize if possible

**Example:**
```
Thank you so much for the kind words, Sophia! Feedback like this makes our day. We're always here if you need anything else.
```

### Frustrated Customers
**All Channels:**
- Acknowledge their frustration first
- Apologize sincerely (if appropriate)
- Focus on solution, not excuses
- Take ownership

**Example (Email):**
```
Hi James,

I completely understand your frustration with the Slack integration issues. I'm sorry this hasn't been working smoothly for you.

Let's get this fixed. I'm escalating your case to our integrations specialist who will reach out within 2 hours with a solution.

In the meantime, I've documented everything you've tried so you won't need to repeat yourself.

Thank you for your patience.

Best,
TechCorp Support
```

**Example (WhatsApp):**
```
I hear you, and I'm sorry this has been frustrating. Let me get our tech team on this right away. You'll hear from a specialist within 2 hours.
```

### Technical Issues
**All Channels:**
- Start with simplest solution
- Use clear, non-technical language when possible
- Provide step-by-step instructions
- Offer escalation if basic troubleshooting doesn't work

**Avoid:**
- Jargon without explanation
- Assuming technical knowledge
- Blaming the customer ("You must have...")

### Billing/Money Issues
**All Channels:**
- Be extra clear and transparent
- Show empathy for financial concerns
- Explain charges in detail
- Provide options when possible

**Example:**
```
I can see why that charge was unexpected. Here's the breakdown:

- Base Professional plan: $240 (10 users × $24)
- Mid-month upgrade prorated: $48 (2 additional users × $24)
- Total: $288

The prorated charge covers the 2 users you added on March 15th. Starting next month, you'll see a consistent charge of $288 for all 12 users.

Does this make sense? Happy to clarify further.
```

### Escalations
**All Channels:**
- Be transparent about escalation
- Set clear expectations for response time
- Summarize what's been discussed
- Reassure customer they'll be taken care of

**Example:**
```
I want to make sure you get the best possible help with this, so I'm connecting you with our [billing/technical/enterprise] team. A specialist will reach out within [timeframe] via [email/phone].

I've shared all the details from our conversation so you won't need to repeat yourself.
```

## Word Choice Guidelines

### Use These Words:
- Help, assist, support
- Understand, clarify, explain
- Solution, resolve, fix
- Happy to, glad to, pleased to
- Appreciate, thank you
- Immediately, right away, quickly

### Avoid These Words:
- Obviously, clearly, simply (can sound condescending)
- Just (minimizes customer's issue)
- Actually (can sound defensive)
- Unfortunately (overused, sounds insincere)
- Impossible, can't, won't (too negative - offer alternatives)
- Honestly, to be honest (implies you're not always honest)

### Replace Negative Phrases:
- ❌ "We can't do that" → ✅ "Here's what we can do instead"
- ❌ "That's not possible" → ✅ "The current options are..."
- ❌ "You should have..." → ✅ "For future reference..."
- ❌ "That's not our policy" → ✅ "Here's how our policy works..."

## Grammar & Formatting

### All Channels:
- Use proper spelling and grammar
- Avoid ALL CAPS (except for emphasis in rare cases)
- Use contractions naturally (we're, you're, it's) - sounds more human
- Proofread before sending

### Email/Web Form:
- Use bullet points or numbered lists for multi-step instructions
- Bold important information sparingly
- Include relevant links
- Use paragraph breaks for readability

### WhatsApp:
- Use line breaks generously
- Keep sentences short
- Numbered lists for steps (but keep brief)
- Minimal formatting

## AI Agent Specific Guidelines

### When AI Should Identify Itself:
- If customer asks "Am I talking to a bot?"
- If escalating to human agent
- If customer seems confused about who they're talking to

**Response:**
```
I'm TechCorp's AI support assistant. I can help with most questions, but I can connect you with a human specialist anytime if you prefer.
```

### When AI Should NOT Identify Itself:
- Proactively in every message (sounds robotic)
- When providing straightforward answers
- When customer is clearly satisfied with the interaction

### Handling Limitations:
If AI doesn't know the answer:
```
That's a great question. Let me connect you with a specialist who can provide the most accurate information. They'll reach out within [timeframe].
```

### Maintaining Consistency:
- Use the same tone throughout the conversation
- Reference previous messages in the thread
- Don't repeat information already provided
- Adapt to customer's communication style

## Examples by Scenario

### Scenario 1: Simple How-To Question
**Gmail:**
```
Hi John,

Great question! You can customize workflow stages in Project Settings > Workflow. From there, you can add, edit, or reorder stages to match your team's process.

Here's a quick guide: [link to documentation]

Let me know if you need any help setting this up!

Best,
TechCorp Support
```

**WhatsApp:**
```
Hi John! Go to Project Settings > Workflow. You can add/edit stages there.

Need help setting it up?
```

### Scenario 2: Billing Concern
**Email:**
```
Hi Laura,

Thanks for asking about mid-month upgrades!

Yes, you can upgrade from Starter to Professional anytime. Here's how billing works:

- You'll be charged the prorated difference for the current month
- Starting next month, you'll pay the full Professional rate
- All charges are automatic - no action needed from you

For example, if you upgrade halfway through the month, you'll pay roughly 50% of the difference between plans, plus your full Professional rate next month.

Want me to process the upgrade for you?

Best,
TechCorp Support
```

**WhatsApp:**
```
Yes! You can upgrade anytime. You'll pay a prorated amount for this month, then the full Professional rate starting next month.

Want me to upgrade your account now?
```

### Scenario 3: Frustrated Customer
**Email:**
```
Hi Karen,

I sincerely apologize for the delay in our response. That's not the level of service we aim to provide, and I understand your frustration.

I'm personally taking ownership of your case and will make sure it's resolved today. Can you share your original ticket number so I can review the issue and get you a solution immediately?

I appreciate your patience and the opportunity to make this right.

Best,
TechCorp Support
```

**WhatsApp:**
```
I'm really sorry for the delay, Karen. That's not okay. Let me personally handle this for you right now.

What was your original issue? I'll get it resolved today.
```

## Quality Checklist

Before sending any response, verify:
- ✅ Answered the customer's question directly
- ✅ Tone matches the channel (formal for email, concise for WhatsApp, balanced for web form)
- ✅ Grammar and spelling are correct
- ✅ No jargon without explanation
- ✅ Clear next steps provided (if applicable)
- ✅ Empathy shown (if customer is frustrated)
- ✅ Professional but warm tone maintained
- ✅ Response length appropriate for channel
