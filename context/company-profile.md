# TechCorp SaaS - Company Profile

## Company Overview
TechCorp SaaS is a B2B software company founded in 2019, providing cloud-based project management and team collaboration tools for small to medium-sized businesses. We serve over 15,000 companies across 40 countries.

## Mission Statement
Empowering teams to work smarter, not harder, through intuitive collaboration tools that scale with your business.

## Core Products

### TaskFlow Pro
Our flagship project management platform featuring:
- Kanban boards and Gantt charts
- Real-time collaboration
- Time tracking and reporting
- Custom workflows and automation
- Mobile apps (iOS/Android)

**Pricing Tiers:**
- Starter: $12/user/month (up to 10 users)
- Professional: $24/user/month (unlimited users)
- Enterprise: Custom pricing (dedicated support, SSO, advanced security)

### TeamChat
Integrated messaging platform with:
- Channels and direct messaging
- File sharing (up to 10GB per workspace)
- Video conferencing (up to 50 participants)
- Screen sharing and recording
- Integration with TaskFlow Pro

**Pricing:**
- Included free with TaskFlow Pro subscriptions
- Standalone: $8/user/month

### Analytics Dashboard
Business intelligence tool providing:
- Project performance metrics
- Team productivity insights
- Custom report builder
- Export to CSV/PDF
- API access for data integration

**Pricing:**
- Professional add-on: $10/user/month
- Included in Enterprise tier
